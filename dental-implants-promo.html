Real Talk

Every Concern We Hear Before a Patient Books

The same questions come up at almost every consultation. Here's what our patients needed to know before they said yes.

Does the implant procedure hurt?
The placement is done under local anesthesia — you feel pressure, not pain. IV sedation and oral sedation are both available if you'd rather sleep through the procedure entirely. Most patients compare recovery to a tooth extraction and return to work the next day.
What if I don't have enough bone?
Bone loss is common after a missing tooth. We handle bone grafting in-house when needed. Our CBCT scan tells us exactly what's possible before any procedure. Most patients qualify even after significant bone loss.
How long will it actually take?
Placement takes 45–90 minutes. Osseointegration takes 3–5 months. Total treatment is typically 4–6 months start to finish. You wear a temporary crown during the healing period — you won't leave with a gap.
How do I know this is worth the cost?
A dental bridge requires grinding down healthy adjacent teeth and typically needs replacement every 10–15 years. An implant preserves adjacent teeth, prevents bone loss, and lasts a lifetime with normal care. The math favors implants over time.
Is the doctor doing the procedure or someone else?
Dr. Shawn or Dr. David Matian personally places every implant in our office. You won't be handed to an associate. You meet the doctor who does your procedure at your first visit.
What if I change my mind after the consultation?
The free consultation is just information. You receive your scan results, a written treatment plan, and exact pricing — and you decide what to do from there, on your own timeline. No pressure, ever.
